Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport and be warmly welcomed by your private chauffeur guide. Transfer to your lakeside hotel in Habarana / Sigiriya. Check in and spend the remainder of the day relaxing after your flight.

🤝 Airport Meet & Greet 🚗 Private Transfer 🏨 Hotel Relaxation
Overnight: Habarana — Wewa Addara Hotel
Meals: Dinner  |  Night 1 of 3

Morning Climb the iconic 5th-century Sigiriya Rock Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage site, famous for its ancient frescoes and panoramic summit views.

Afternoon Experience authentic rural life on an immersive Village Tour featuring a catamaran ride, traditional Sri Lankan cooking demonstration, and village lunch.

🥾 Sigiriya Rock 🌾 Village Tour
Overnight: Habarana — Wewa Addara Hotel
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner  |  Night 2 of 3

Morning & Afternoon Enjoy a leisurely morning at your resort, taking in the serene natural surroundings or relaxing by the pool.

Evening Revitalize your senses with an authentic traditional Sri Lankan Ayurveda Massage at a nearby wellness centre.

🌿 Ayurveda Massage 🌴 Resort Relaxation
Overnight: Habarana — Wewa Addara Hotel
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner  |  Night 3 of 3

Check out and travel towards the hill capital of Kandy. En route, visit the magnificent Dambulla Cave Temple complex and a Matale Spice Garden. In Kandy, pay homage at the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic, followed by an evening Kandyan Cultural Show.

🛕 Dambulla Cave Temple 🌶️ Spice Garden 🛕 Temple of the Tooth 💃 Cultural Show
Overnight: Kandy — Topaz Hotel
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Visit a licensed Gem Museum in Kandy before driving up into the tea country. Tour a working Ceylon Tea Factory & Plantation to observe tea plucking and processing, stop at the scenic Ramboda Falls, and arrive in the cool hill station of Nuwara Eliya.

💎 Gem Museum ☕ Tea Factory & Plantation 🌊 Ramboda Falls
Overnight: Nuwara Eliya — Araliya Red Hotel
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Board the famous highland train for a world-renowned scenic train journey winding through misty mountain passes, pine forests, and emerald tea valleys to Ella. Check in to your hotel and unwind with beautiful valley views.

🚂 Scenic Train Ride 🏔️ Ella Gap Views
Overnight: Ella — Hillcrest Ella
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Descend from Ella towards Tissamaharama. In the afternoon, set out on an exciting 4x4 Jeep Safari through Yala National Park, famous worldwide for its wild elephant herds, sloth bears, birds, and high concentration of leopards.

🐆 Yala Safari 🐘 4x4 Wildlife Jeep Safari
Overnight: Tissamaharama / Yala — Hotel Tamarind Tree
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Drive along the southern coast to explore the historic UNESCO World Heritage site Galle Fort. Enjoy a scenic boat safari through the mangroves of Madu River or Koggala Lagoon, and visit a Sea Turtle Hatchery supporting marine conservation.

🏰 UNESCO Galle Fort 🚤 Madu River / Koggala Lagoon 🐢 Sea Turtle Hatchery
Overnight: Down South — Pandanus Beach Resort & Spa
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast, check out from your hotel and take a private express highway transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport in Katunayake (or Negombo) for your departure flight home, bringing your memorable Sri Lankan holiday to a close.

Departure Transfer: Private transfer to airport.
Meals: Breakfast
